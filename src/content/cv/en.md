## What I do

Full-stack development, from low-level systems up to interfaces people actually enjoy
using, with a specialisation in digital forensics.

The common thread across everything here is a preference for software that keeps what it
handles: minimal dependencies, reproducible builds, and architectures that do not need a
privacy policy because they never collect anything in the first place.

## Skills

**Languages.** Rust for systems work. Python for tooling and artefact parsing. Kotlin for
Android. PHP and JavaScript for the web.

**Frontend.** HTML, CSS, React, Vue. Accessible markup and interfaces that work without
JavaScript where that is achievable.

**Backend and infrastructure.** Node.js, MySQL, Linux, Android, Git. Self-hosted services,
dependency-light by design.

**Digital forensics.** Artefact parsing, evidence handling, and the tooling around it.

**Applied cryptography.** End-to-end encrypted messaging, key agreement, and the failure
modes that matter more than the happy path — what a system does when a key expires or a
peer disappears.

## What I can help with

Practical work for individuals: recovering what a device still holds, understanding what
an application is actually sending, de-Googling a phone, hardening a personal setup, or
building a small tool that does one thing without phoning home.

If you are not sure whether something falls in scope, ask. A short honest answer costs
nothing.

## Selected work

**Seca** — three communication apps for Android: contacts, calls and messages. One design,
no Google dependency, and end-to-end encrypted messaging between Seca phones over public
Nostr relays. Written in Kotlin.
